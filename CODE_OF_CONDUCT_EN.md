# Code of Conduct

English | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

We want this project to remain open, professional, and welcoming. When joining discussions, opening Issues, or contributing code:

- Respect people with different experience levels and perspectives;
- Focus discussions on verifiable technical facts;
- Avoid harassment, insults, discrimination, and disclosure of personal information;
- Do not post real conversations, account details, or other sensitive data;
- Do not promote unauthorized monitoring, data collection, or privacy violations.

To report a conduct issue, use a private contact option provided by GitHub. If no private channel is available, open an Issue without sensitive details and ask the maintainers to follow up.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content and limit or reject further participation when necessary.
